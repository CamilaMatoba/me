Calendars on DAX

Camila MatobaMarch 7, 2022 · 2 min read

For a capacity-planning tool, I needed to account for working days (Monday to Friday), ISO weeks, and public holidays in several countries. I made a Power BI calendar that can be exported to CSV, with holidays for three countries over five years.

The original project included six countries, but loading 30 web queries (six countries by five years) took a long time. I would not recommend scaling the Power BI query much beyond that.

ISO week

The calendar uses ISO week numbering, where Monday starts the week:

Week = YEAR('Calendar'[Date] - WEEKNUM('Calendar'[Date], 21) + 26)
       & "/" & FORMAT(WEEKNUM('Calendar'[Date], 21), "00")

For reliable date logic, test week-year boundaries around New Year's. ISO week numbers can belong to the previous or next calendar year.

Holiday data

The original file includes 2022–2026 public holidays for the Netherlands, Germany, and the United States. Country and year are variables in Power Query, so you can adapt the list.
